IN-DEPTH MARKETING COURSE
=========================
A complete system for research, positioning, acquisition, conversion, retention and measurement.

PART 1 - MARKET RESEARCH & POSITIONING
1.1 Customer research
  - Run 10-15 interviews. Ask: "What were you doing when you decided to look for a solution?" and "What else did you try?"
  - Mine reviews of competitors (3-star reviews are the most honest). Record repeated phrases.
  - Build a Jobs-To-Be-Done statement: "When [situation], I want to [motivation], so I can [outcome]."
1.2 Competitor map
  - List 5-8 alternatives (including "do nothing"). Note price, promise, weakness.
  - Find the gap: an audience or promise nobody owns.
1.3 Positioning
  - Category, target, key benefit, proof, and why you are different.
  - Test it: can a stranger repeat what you do after 5 seconds on your homepage?

PART 2 - OFFER, PRICING & MESSAGING
2.1 Offer design: outcome, timeframe, risk reversal (guarantee), bonuses that remove objections.
2.2 Pricing: anchor with a higher tier, offer 3 tiers, aim the middle at your ideal customer. Raise prices when more than 80% of prospects say yes without hesitation.
2.3 Messaging framework: Problem -> Cost of inaction -> Solution -> Proof -> Call to action.
2.4 Copywriting rules: one idea per sentence, concrete numbers, customer language, active voice.

PART 3 - THE FUNNEL
Awareness -> Interest -> Consideration -> Purchase -> Retention -> Referral.
3.1 Lead magnet: a small, specific win (checklist, template, calculator), delivered instantly.
3.2 Landing page conversion checklist: single goal, headline matches ad/source, above-the-fold call to action, social proof near the button, fast load on mobile, minimal form fields.
3.3 Sales page anatomy: headline, story of the problem, solution, how it works, proof, offer stack, guarantee, FAQ, final call to action.

PART 4 - ACQUISITION CHANNELS
4.1 SEO: pick keywords by intent (problem, comparison, purchase). One page per search topic; earn links with original data and useful tools; update top pages every 6 months.
4.2 Content: one pillar piece per month, split into 10+ smaller posts; always end with a next step.
4.3 Email: welcome sequence (5 emails: story, quick win, proof, objection, offer), weekly newsletter, re-engagement after 60 days of silence. Watch open rate, click rate, unsubscribe rate.
4.4 Paid ads: start at $20-50/day, test 3 creatives x 2 audiences, judge on cost per acquisition after 50+ clicks. Scale winners by 20% at a time. Retarget visitors who did not buy.
4.5 Partnerships & affiliates: find businesses with your audience but not your product; offer a revenue share.
4.6 Outbound: short, specific, about them; follow up 3-4 times over 2 weeks.

PART 5 - CONVERSION & RETENTION
5.1 Onboarding: get the customer to their first win in under 24 hours.
5.2 Retention: check-ins at day 3, 14 and 45; gather feedback; fix the top complaint each month.
5.3 Referrals: ask right after a success moment; reward both sides.
5.4 Upsell: offer the next logical step only after delivering results.

PART 6 - METRICS & TESTING
- Know your numbers: CAC (customer acquisition cost), LTV (lifetime value), conversion rate, payback period, churn.
- Healthy target: LTV at least 3x CAC; payback in under 12 months.
- A/B test one change at a time; run until roughly 100 conversions per variant; keep a testing log.
- Weekly dashboard: traffic, leads, sales, revenue, cost per lead, cost per sale.

PART 7 - 90-DAY ROADMAP
Days 1-14: Research, positioning, offer, landing page, tracking set up.
Days 15-30: Launch lead magnet, welcome emails, and one main channel.
Days 31-60: Add a second channel, publish pillar content, start paid tests.
Days 61-90: Optimize funnel by data, launch referral program, raise prices if demand allows, document what works.

FINAL PRINCIPLE: Understand the customer deeper than competitors, make a clear offer, measure everything, and improve one thing at a time.
