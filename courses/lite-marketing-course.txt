LITE MARKETING COURSE
=====================
Goal: get your first reliable stream of customers in 30 days.

MODULE 1 - KNOW WHO YOU SERVE
- Pick ONE specific customer (e.g. "freelance photographers", not "small businesses").
- Write down their top 3 problems in THEIR words. Get these from reviews, forums and 5 short conversations.
- Positioning sentence: "I help [customer] get [result] without [pain]."

MODULE 2 - BUILD A CLEAR OFFER
- An offer = result + timeframe + price + guarantee.
- Example: "A 5-page website in 7 days for $900, revised until you're happy."
- Price on value delivered, not on hours worked.

MODULE 3 - THE LANDING PAGE FORMULA
1. Headline: the result the customer wants.
2. Subheadline: how you deliver it, in one line.
3. Three benefits (not features).
4. Proof: testimonials, numbers, logos, or a short case study.
5. One clear button. One action per page.

MODULE 4 - PICK TWO CHANNELS
Choose two and ignore the rest until they work:
- Search (SEO): slow, cheap, compounding.
- Social: needs steady posting; best for personality-led brands.
- Email: best return on investment; start a list from day one.
- Direct outreach: fastest way to first sales.
- Paid ads: fast but costs money; only after your offer converts.

MODULE 5 - 30-DAY STARTER PLAN
Week 1: Choose customer, write offer, build landing page.
Week 2: Set up email capture and a welcome email. Post/outreach daily.
Week 3: Contact 10 prospects per day; publish 2 helpful pieces of content.
Week 4: Review results, keep what worked, drop what didn't, repeat.

RULE OF THUMB: Consistency beats cleverness. Ship, measure, improve.
